PF, ESI and professional tax, registered and filed every month.

The rules for employees’ benefits are strict about dates. We register your establishment, calculate every contribution and file on time, so your team’s benefits and your compliance stay intact.

Ask about PF, ESI and professional taxWhat we do

Once you have employees, you have obligations to them and to the authorities: provident fund, state insurance, professional tax in many states, and the registers that go with them. The contributions are due every month, usually by the 15th, and the penalties for delay are charged as interest and damages.

Apbiz handles registration and the monthly cycle, and keeps the records in a form an inspector would accept.

What we do

EPF registration and employer codes

Establishment registration, employer and employee portal access and the UAN for each employee.

Monthly EPF contribution and ECR

Calculation, payment and electronic return filing by the due date.

ESI registration and contributions

Employer registration, insurance numbers for employees and monthly contribution payment.

Professional tax

Enrolment and registration and periodic returns in the states that levy it, matched to each state’s rates and dates.

Employee changes

Joining and exit entries, transfers of accounts and corrections to names, dates and wages.

Claims and inspections

Help with claims and responses to notices or inspections.

Who this is for

  • Employers who have reached the headcount where registration is required
  • Businesses that hire their first employees
  • Companies that operate in several states
  • Employers who received an inspection or damages notice

How it works

  1. 1

    Assess

  2. 2

    We apply, activate your portal logins and set up your employees.

  3. 3

  • PAN, incorporation or partnership papers

  • Digital signature of the authorised person

Frequently asked questions

When does PF registration become mandatory?

Generally when an establishment reaches the prescribed number of employees. Smaller employers can register voluntarily, and many do because it helps with hiring.

When are PF and ESI contributions due?

Typically by the 15th of the following month. We track the date and prepare the challan before it.

Does every state levy professional tax?

No. It is a state tax, so whether it applies and at what rate depends on where your employees work. We check the states you operate in.

What if I paid late?

Interest and damages are levied for the period of delay. Pay as early as possible and tell us, and we will compute what is due and help with any notice.

Will you also run my payroll?

Yes. Payroll and these statutory payments belong together, and we offer them as a single service.

This page is general information and not advice for your situation. Rates, limits and due dates change, and some depend on your state or scheme, so we confirm the current rules for your case before we act.
