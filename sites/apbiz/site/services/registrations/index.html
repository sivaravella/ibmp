Business registrations and licences, sorted.

The right registrations open doors: bank loans, government schemes, imports and exports, and the trust of larger customers. We find which ones you need and get them done.

Ask about Registrations and licencesWhat we do

Running a business in India often needs more than one registration, and the list depends on what you do, where you do it and how big you are. Some are free and quick, some depend on your state or city, and some must be renewed.

We tell you what applies to you, prepare the applications and keep track of the renewals.

What we do

Udyam registration (MSME)

The online registration that unlocks priority-sector lending and a range of government benefits for micro, small and medium enterprises.

Shops and Establishment

The state registration for shops, offices and commercial establishments, with renewals and amendments.

FSSAI food licence

Registration or licence for food businesses, matched to your size and activities, with renewals.

Import-Export Code (IEC)

The code needed to import or export goods, with updates when details change.

PAN and TAN

New applications and corrections.

Trade licences and local permissions

Municipal and other local approvals that apply to your premises and activity.

GST and professional tax enrolments

Registered for you as part of the same set-up, so everything is aligned from the start.

Who this is for

  • New businesses setting up their first office or shop
  • Food businesses, restaurants and caterers
  • Importers, exporters and e-commerce sellers
  • MSMEs that want credit or scheme benefits

Frequently asked questions

Is Udyam registration compulsory?

It is not compulsory for running a business, but it is needed for many benefits for small enterprises, so most eligible businesses register.

Which FSSAI licence do I need?

It depends on the type of food business and its turnover or capacity. We check which category applies and apply for it.

Do I need an IEC to export services?

An Import-Export Code is needed to import or export goods. For services the rules are lighter, but some incentives and banking transactions still ask for it. We confirm for your case.

Are registrations done by the state or the centre?

Both. Udyam and IEC are central, while Shops and Establishment, trade licences and professional tax are state or local. We handle each at its own office or portal.

How long does registration take?

It varies by registration and by state and by how quickly documents are in order. We tell you what to expect before we start.

This page is general information and not advice for your situation. Rates, limits and due dates change, and some depend on your state or scheme, so we confirm the current rules for your case before we act.
