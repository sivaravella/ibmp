Payroll that is correct every month.

Salaries are the one payment your team notices immediately. We calculate them accurately, pay the statutory dues on time and give each employee a clear payslip.

Ask about PayrollWhat we do

Payroll sits where accounting, tax and labour law meet. A salary structure affects how much tax an employee pays, how much you contribute, and what a settlement looks like when someone leaves. Getting it wrong shows up first as unhappy employees and later as notices.

Apbiz designs the salary structure, runs payroll each month and takes care of every deduction and payment that follows from it.

What we do

Salary structure design

Basic, allowances and reimbursements arranged so they are compliant and tax-efficient for the employee.

Monthly payroll processing

Attendance and leave, earnings, deductions, arrears and the net pay for each person.

Payslips and registers

Clear payslips for every employee and the payroll registers you need to keep.

Statutory deductions and payments

PF, ESI, professional tax and tax deducted from salary, calculated and paid on time.

Form 16 and the annual cycle

Year-end tax computation for each employee and Form 16 for the whole team.

Bonus, gratuity and leave encashment

Calculations as and when they fall due.

Joining and exit

Offer-letter checks, onboarding entries and full-and-final settlements.

Who this is for

  • Growing teams that outgrew spreadsheets
  • Startups that need payroll without a full-time HR person
  • Companies in several states with different rules
  • Businesses that want a clean audit trail for salaries

Frequently asked questions

What do you need from me each month?

The changes: joiners, leavers, attendance or leave and any one-time payments. We take it from there.

Do you also pay the statutory dues?

We calculate and prepare them, and file the related returns. The payment itself is made from your account on your approval, unless you ask us to arrange it differently.

Can you handle employees in different states?

Yes. State rules for professional tax and for registration differ, and we apply the right ones to each location.

How do employees declare their investments for tax?

We collect declarations at the start of the year, adjust the deduction through the months and issue Form 16 after the year-end.

What happens when someone resigns?

We calculate the final settlement: salary, leave encashment, bonus or gratuity if due, and recoveries, and update every statutory record.
