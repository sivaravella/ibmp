The dates that matter.

The recurring deadlines most Indian businesses face. We track the ones that apply to you.

WhatWhen
GSTR-1 (monthly filers)11th of the next month
GSTR-3B (monthly filers)20th of the next month
TDS / TCS payment7th of the next month
PF and ESI contributions15th of the next month
Advance tax15 June, 15 September, 15 December, 15 March
Income tax return (non-audit cases)31 July
Tax audit report30 September
Director KYC (DIR-3 KYC)30 September
ROC annual filings (AOC-4, MGT-7)30 and 60 days after the AGM

Due dates are extended or changed by the government from time to time, and some depend on your state or filing scheme. Confirm your own dates with us before relying on this list.
